How it works

Four steps to
inbox zero.

  1. We connect to your Microsoft 365 mailbox via secure API access
  2. AI reads each incoming email, classifies it, and routes it to the right folder
  3. At 7am each morning you get a summary email grouped by category
  4. We refine the classification with your feedback over 2 weeks until accuracy is tuned

Real project

Built for an Australian
Manufacturer

A finance manager monitoring two high-volume email accounts across two business entities. Important emails — supplier invoices, urgent orders, warranty claims — were mixed with routine correspondence. Critical items were being missed.

We built an AI triage system that classifies every incoming email into six categories (Priority, Financial, Warranty, Marketing, HR, Needs Review), routes them to the correct Outlook folder, and delivers a morning summary at 7am. Two rounds of client-led refinement tuned the accuracy to the client's specific needs.

Common questions

Before you
get started.

What email systems does this work with?

Currently Microsoft 365 and Outlook. The system connects via Microsoft Graph API, so any M365 mailbox is supported.

How accurate is the AI classification?

After 2 weeks of tuning with your feedback, accuracy is typically 90%+ for well-defined categories. Emails the AI isn't sure about go to a "Needs Review" folder.

Can it handle multiple email accounts?

Yes. We can run the same system across as many mailboxes as you need, each with account-specific routing rules.

How long does it take to set up?

Typically 2 weeks — one week for the build and one week for tuning and refinement based on your feedback.

What does ongoing maintenance cost?

Monthly maintenance covers monitoring, workflow health checks, and adjustments as your categories evolve. We'll give you a clear monthly figure upfront.
