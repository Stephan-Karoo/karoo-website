Before you get in touch

Common questions
answered.

Where is Karoo based?

Karoo is based in Melbourne, Victoria. We serve clients across Australia and globally, including New Zealand and South Africa.

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day. For urgent matters, call us directly on 0434 636 532.

Do you work with businesses outside Melbourne?

Yes. While we're based in Melbourne, most of our work is delivered remotely. We have clients across Victoria and interstate.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We'll review your message and get back to you within one business day. If it looks like a good fit, we'll suggest a 15-minute call to understand what you're trying to achieve before recommending anything.

Do you offer ongoing support or one-off projects?

Both. Some clients engage us for a single project — a website build or an ad campaign setup. Most end up staying on for ongoing support across one or more service areas.
