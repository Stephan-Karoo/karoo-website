Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Karoo (operated by Karoo Media Pty Ltd, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information when you use our website, services, and client portal.

1. Who We Are

Karoo Media Pty Ltd operates under the trading names Karoo, Karoo Digital Media, Karoo Digital Marketing, Karoo IT Services, and Karoo AI Automation. Our registered business address is Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. You can contact us at hello@karoo.com.au.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details you provide via enquiry forms (name, email, phone, business name)
  • Usage data when you visit our website (pages viewed, time on site, referring URL) via Google Analytics
  • Advertising performance data from platforms including Google Ads and Meta Ads, on behalf of our clients
  • Login credentials and activity within the Karoo Client Portal (portal.karoo.com.au)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and deliver agreed services
  • Manage and report on digital advertising campaigns on behalf of clients
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Communicate service updates and relevant information to clients

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Platforms and APIs

As part of our services, we integrate with third-party platforms including Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, and associated APIs. Data accessed through these integrations is used solely for reporting and campaign management purposes on behalf of our clients. We comply with Google's API Services User Data Policy and Meta's Platform Terms.

5. Client Portal

The Karoo Client Portal stores advertising performance data securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL). Access is restricted to authorised users only. We do not share client data between separate client accounts.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics and performance purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain client data for as long as required to deliver our services, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at hello@karoo.com.au.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at karoo.com.au/privacy.
