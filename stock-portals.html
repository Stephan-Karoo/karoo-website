Real project

Built for an Australian
Suspension Kit Manufacturer

A manufacturer managing stock across three physical locations using spreadsheets. Orders came in via email and phone. Production was tracked on Monday.com with no connection to their ordering system. Invoices were created manually in Xero.

We built a centralised portal connecting their eCommerce store, accounting system, production tracker, and warehouse operations. Orders flow in, stock updates automatically, invoices generate in Xero, and production items appear on the Monday.com factory board.

Common questions

Before you
get started.

Can this connect to my existing accounting software?

Yes. We integrate directly with Xero and can connect to MYOB and other platforms via API.

How many products can the system handle?

There's no practical limit. Our largest portal manages 970+ products across 17 categories with 3,400+ BOM component relationships.

Do I need to change my current tools?

No. The portal connects to what you already use — Xero, Monday.com, Shopify, WooCommerce. That's the point.

How long does a typical build take?

Depends on complexity. A full portal with integrations typically takes 10–16 weeks. Simpler setups can be faster.

What happens after launch?

Monthly maintenance covers system monitoring, workflow health checks, minor updates, and support. Your systems don't just launch — they stay healthy.
