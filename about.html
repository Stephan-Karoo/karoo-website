About Karoo

One team.
Four divisions.
Your entire stack.

We're a Melbourne-based systems agency. We build the web, run the ads, hold the infrastructure, and automate whatever's left — for operators who need it to actually work.

Our story

Started with a WordPress site in 2009. Never really stopped.

Karoo began as a freelance web development practice. Over 15 years, the work grew into something more: clients started asking us to run their ads, manage their servers, and eventually automate the manual work that was costing them hours every week.

Now we operate four specialist divisions under one roof. Each one is a discrete capability. Together, they cover the full digital stack — from the surface to the systems underneath it.

We work with local operators across Melbourne — childcare centres, trades businesses, manufacturers, professional services firms, and food brands — who want digital infrastructure that performs, not just exists.

Our principles

How we work.

01

Scope before you build

Every project starts with a written scope. No discovery fees buried in a retainer. You know what you're getting and what it costs before we start.

02

Build in the open

Weekly demos. Shared staging environments. You can see the work as it happens — not at a grand reveal six weeks after kickoff.

03

Own what you pay for

You get the code, the accounts, the data. No vendor lock-in. No proprietary systems you can't leave. If we part ways, you take everything with you.

04

Stay on the stack

We don't build and disappear. Retainer clients get monitoring, iteration, and a direct line. Infrastructure needs maintenance — we provide it.

05

Direct access, always

You speak to the person doing the work. Stephan handles every project directly. That's how quality stays consistent and timelines stay honest.

06

Measure everything

Campaigns have dashboards. Servers have monitoring. Workflows have logs. If it's running, we know how it's performing — and so do you.
