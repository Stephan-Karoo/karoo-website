About Karoo

We're a Melbourne-based systems agency. We build the web, run the ads, hold the infrastructure, and automate whatever's left — for operators who need it to actually work.

Our story

Built businesses through digital marketing. Now building the systems that run them.

I've been helping businesses grow through digital channels for over 15 years. Started with Google Ads and lead generation for local clients across Melbourne. That grew into websites, email systems, CRM setup, cloud infrastructure — whatever clients needed to actually convert and close leads.

In 2020, I formalised the operation as Karoo Media and structured it into four focused divisions: Digital Marketing, Digital Media, IT Services, and AI Automation. Same work, better systems.

Most clients come for one thing — usually ads or a website — and stay because the full-service model works. Marketing generates leads. IT infrastructure keeps systems running. AI automation removes manual work. Media brings it together.

I work directly with clients. No account managers, no project coordinators. If you're working with Karoo, you're working with me.

Client industries include childcare centres, manufacturing, trades, food services, and eCommerce. The common thread: businesses that need leads, systems, and growth — not just a website.

Our principles

How we work.

01

Scope before you build

Every project starts with a written scope. No discovery fees buried in a retainer. You know what you're getting and what it costs before we start.

02

Build in the open

Weekly demos. Shared staging environments. You can see the work as it happens — not at a grand reveal six weeks after kickoff.

03

Own what you pay for

You get the code, the accounts, the data. No vendor lock-in. No proprietary systems you can't leave. If we part ways, you take everything with you.

04

Stay on the stack

We don't build and disappear. Retainer clients get monitoring, iteration, and a direct line. Infrastructure needs maintenance — we provide it.

05

Direct access, always

You speak to the person doing the work. Stephan handles every project directly. That's how quality stays consistent and timelines stay honest.

06

Measure everything

Campaigns have dashboards. Servers have monitoring. Workflows have logs. If it's running, we know how it's performing — and so do you.
