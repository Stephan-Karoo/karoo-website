Case Study

Manufacturing
Stock Portal

A centralised stock management system connecting Xero, Monday.com, and WooCommerce for an Australian suspension kit manufacturer. 970+ products across 3 warehouse locations.

Overview

Replacing spreadsheets
with a real system.

An Australian suspension kit manufacturer needed to move from spreadsheet-based stock tracking to a centralised operations portal. Stock was managed across three physical warehouse locations with no single source of truth. Orders came in via email and phone, production was tracked on Monday.com with no connection to the ordering system, and invoices were created manually in Xero.

We built a custom React-based portal that connects their eCommerce store, accounting system, production tracker, and warehouse operations into one system.

The challenge

Multiple systems.
No connection.

  • Stock tracked in spreadsheets across 3 warehouse locations
  • No real-time visibility into stock levels for sales or warehouse staff
  • Orders arriving via email, phone, and WooCommerce with no unified process
  • Manual invoice creation in Xero for every order
  • Production tracked on Monday.com with no link to orders or stock
  • No low-stock alerts — reordering was reactive, not proactive
  • BOM (Bill of Materials) relationships managed manually
  • No role-based access — everyone saw everything or nothing

What we built

A complete operations
portal.

  • Centralised stock dashboard with real-time levels across all 3 locations
  • Order management system with basket flow and per-account pricing
  • BOM tracking — ordering a kit automatically reserves every component
  • Xero integration — invoices generated automatically on order approval
  • Monday.com integration — approved orders create production items on factory boards
  • WooCommerce sync — online shop stock stays in sync with portal levels
  • Low-stock alerts with configurable thresholds per SKU
  • Role-based access for admin, warehouse, and sales staff
  • Product management with categories, images, and pricing tiers
  • Order approval workflow with status tracking
  • Stock transfer between warehouse locations
  • Reporting and export functionality

Key numbers

The system at
a glance.

970+ Products managed
17 Product categories
3 Warehouse locations
3,400+ BOM relationships
500+ SKU alert thresholds
14wk Build timeline

The approach

Documentation-first
methodology.

  1. Mapped the complete data flow — where stock lives, how orders move, where invoices go, how production is tracked
  2. Full system architecture and database schema documented before any code was written
  3. Phased build over 14 weeks with a shared progress tracker — the client could see exactly where we were at any point
  4. Integration testing with Xero, Monday.com, and WooCommerce in a staging environment before go-live
  5. Go-live with staff training, then ongoing monthly maintenance covering monitoring, health checks, and updates
