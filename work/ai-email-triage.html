Case Study

AI Email Triage
& Morning Summary

An AI-powered email classification system that sorts, routes, and summarises incoming email across two business accounts for a manufacturer's finance team.

Overview

Turning inbox chaos
into structured data.

A finance manager at an Australian manufacturer was monitoring two high-volume email accounts across two business entities. Important emails — supplier invoices, urgent orders, warranty claims — were mixed with routine correspondence, marketing emails, and internal notifications. Critical items were being missed or delayed.

We built an AI triage system using n8n and Claude that classifies every incoming email, routes it to the correct Outlook folder, and delivers a structured morning summary at 7am each day.

The challenge

Two inboxes.
No structure.

  • Two business email accounts monitored by one finance manager
  • Important emails buried under newsletters, marketing, and routine mail
  • No way to quickly identify urgent items without reading every email
  • Supplier invoices, warranty claims, and orders all mixed together
  • Critical items missed or delayed because they weren't seen in time
  • 30+ minutes spent each morning manually sorting and forwarding email

What we built

AI classification
and routing.

  • AI-powered email classification using Claude API
  • Automated folder routing via Microsoft Graph API
  • Morning summary digest delivered at 7am, grouped by category
  • Multi-account support — same system across both business email addresses
  • Custom classification categories tuned to the client's business
  • "Needs Review" fallback for emails the AI isn't confident about

The approach

Build, test, tune
with feedback.

  1. Connected to both Microsoft 365 mailboxes via secure Microsoft Graph API access
  2. Built the n8n workflow to read each incoming email and pass it to Claude AI for classification
  3. Configured automated folder routing — each category maps to a specific Outlook folder
  4. Built the 7am morning summary that groups overnight emails by category and delivers a structured overview
  5. Two rounds of client-led refinement over 2 weeks to tune classification accuracy to the client's specific needs
